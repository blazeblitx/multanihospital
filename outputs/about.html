ABOUT THE CENTRE

A personal approach
to surgical care.

Multani Plastic Surgery Centre is a private practice in Jalandhar led by Dr. Jangpreet Singh Multani. The centre provides consultations and care across plastic, cosmetic and reconstructive surgery, with services including hair restoration, laser treatments and other procedures. Patients can discuss their concerns, suitable options and next steps with the care team.

Care shaped around
your conversation.

The clinic describes its approach as personalized, with discussion of each patient’s concerns, available treatments and next steps.

Its About page lists hair-loss care, laser hair removal, scar treatment, chemical peels, anti-wrinkle treatments, fillers, anti-ageing treatments and reconstructive surgeries among its treatment areas.
