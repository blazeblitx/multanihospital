OTHER SERVICES

Hand & Micro Vascular Surgery

Hand and microvascular surgery concerns conditions or injuries involving the hand and fine blood vessels. The centre lists this topic among its services. A specialist can assess the specific problem and explain possible repair or reconstruction, expected function, timing, risks, rehabilitation and the care pathway for your case.

OTHER SERVICES

Congenital Anomalies

Congenital anomalies are conditions in body structures that are present from birth. The centre’s service information discusses surgical care for some such conditions. The appropriate plan depends on the diagnosis, age, function and individual needs; a specialist can explain assessment, possible procedures, timing, risks and follow-up.

OTHER SERVICES

Maxillofacial surgery

Maxillofacial surgery addresses conditions affecting the face, jaws and related structures. The centre lists it among its other services. Assessment depends on the concern and may involve discussion of function as well as appearance. Ask the specialist about diagnostic steps, treatment options, recovery, risks and follow-up.

OTHER SERVICES

Burns Treatment

Burns care depends on the burn’s depth, size, location and when it occurred. The centre lists burns treatment among its services. A clinician can assess healing and discuss wound care or reconstructive options when appropriate. For a new or serious burn, seek urgent medical care rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

Contact the clinic to confirm current availability and discuss your circumstances with the care team.
