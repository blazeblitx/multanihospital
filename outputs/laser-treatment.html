LASER TREATMENT

Laser Hair Reduction

Laser hair reduction uses light-based treatment directed at hair follicles to reduce unwanted hair over a course of sessions. The centre lists this service among its laser treatments. Ask about skin and hair suitability, preparation, expected session spacing, possible skin reactions, aftercare and whether another approach may be appropriate.

LASER TREATMENT

Fractional CO2 LASER

Fractional CO₂ laser treatment is a resurfacing procedure that delivers laser energy in small areas of the skin. The centre lists it in connection with scar marks. A clinician can explain whether it fits your concern, expected recovery and aftercare, risks such as pigment changes, and available alternatives.

Contact the clinic to confirm current availability and discuss your circumstances with the care team.
