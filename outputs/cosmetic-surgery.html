COSMETIC SURGERY

Liposuction

Liposuction is a surgical procedure that removes localized fat to alter body contour; it is not a weight-loss treatment. The clinic lists it among its cosmetic surgery services. A surgeon can discuss goals, suitability, possible complications, scars, recovery time and whether non-surgical or other surgical options should be considered.

COSMETIC SURGERY

Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck)

Abdominoplasty, often called a tummy tuck, is surgery that removes excess abdominal skin and may tighten underlying tissues. The clinic lists it as a cosmetic procedure. Ask the surgeon about the proposed technique, scar placement, recovery, risks, expected changes and whether your health and goals make surgery appropriate.

COSMETIC SURGERY

Rhinoplasty or Nose Job

Rhinoplasty is surgery to change the shape or structure of the nose. The clinic’s service list also calls it a nose job. A consultation can clarify your goals, what changes may be realistic, breathing considerations, possible risks, recovery and whether surgery is a suitable option for your individual circumstances.

COSMETIC SURGERY

Scar Revision

Scar revision refers to procedures intended to change the appearance or function of a scar. The right approach depends on the scar, its location, healing history and your goals; complete removal cannot be guaranteed. Ask the surgeon about likely improvement, risks, recovery and non-surgical alternatives.

COSMETIC SURGERY

Blepharoplasty/Eyelid surgery

Blepharoplasty is eyelid surgery that may address excess skin or tissue around the upper or lower eyelids. The clinic lists it as an eyelid procedure. A surgeon can assess your concerns and eye health, then discuss the proposed approach, potential effects, risks, recovery and other options.

COSMETIC SURGERY

Fat Transfer Procedure

Fat transfer moves a patient’s own fat from one area of the body to another. The clinic lists this as a cosmetic procedure. Ask the surgeon how fat would be collected and placed, what changes may be expected, how results can vary, and what risks, recovery and alternatives apply.

COSMETIC SURGERY

Facelift Procedure

A facelift is surgery intended to address visible changes in the face and jawline associated with ageing. The clinic lists a facelift procedure among its cosmetic services. A consultation can discuss your goals, likely limits, technique, scars, recovery, risks and whether other treatments may better fit your needs.

Contact the clinic to confirm current availability and discuss your circumstances with the care team.
