WEBSITE INFORMATION

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Based on a review of the site files, the website itself does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Essential browser storage may be used to remember your cookie choice. Hosting infrastructure may use technically necessary storage or logs; confirm this with the host.

Third-party content

Google Maps, Google Fonts, Facebook/Instagram and video providers can receive connection data and may set or read cookies or similar technologies. External images can also make a connection to their host. Provider behavior may change. The map and fonts currently load with the page; video and selected social content may connect when opened.

Your choice

You can accept optional third-party content or decline it. Declining leaves links to open content directly with its provider. You can also manage cookies in your browser. Blocking all storage may prevent the site from remembering your preference.

Cookie rules depend on the visitor's location and the technology used. Visitors in regions requiring prior consent should not have non-essential trackers or embeds loaded before valid consent. Current review found no analytics/advertising trackers, but third-party map/font connections happen before a choice; this implementation lets you decline these connections for the current visit, and a production review should verify all network requests and consent law applicability.

See the Privacy Policy for more about third-party data handling.
