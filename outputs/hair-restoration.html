Hair restoration begins with an individual assessment and a conversation about your concerns and goals. The centre’s public service information includes hair transplant surgery for male pattern baldness.

HAIR RESTORATION

FUE Treatment

Follicular unit extraction (FUE) is a hair-transplant technique that harvests individual follicular units from a donor area for placement in areas of hair loss. The clinic’s article discusses donor harvesting and the treatment process. A consultation can cover suitability, planning, recovery, risks and realistic expectations.

HAIR RESTORATION

PRP Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy is described by the clinic as a non-surgical hair-loss treatment. Its article explains that a blood sample is processed to prepare plasma for injection. Ask the surgeon about the evidence, whether it may suit your situation, possible risks, the number of visits and what outcomes to expect.

HAIR RESTORATION

Low Level Laser Therapy

Low-level laser therapy is listed among the centre’s hair-restoration services. It uses low-intensity light as the basis of treatment. Ask the clinic what device and schedule it currently offers, what evidence applies to your concern, and how benefits, limitations, risks and other options would be assessed for you.

HAIR RESTORATION

Mesotherapy

The clinic’s mesotherapy article discusses injections containing selected vitamins and other substances for cosmetic and hair-related concerns. The ingredients and treatment plan can vary. Before considering it, ask the clinician what substances are proposed, what evidence supports their use, possible side effects, how many sessions may be involved and what alternatives exist.

Contact the clinic to confirm current availability and discuss your circumstances with the care team.
