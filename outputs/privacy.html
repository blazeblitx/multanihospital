Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This notice describes information handled through this website. It is not a notice for clinical records or care delivered at the centre; those are handled under separate applicable requirements.

Who operates this site

Multani Plastic Surgery Centre, SCO 97, Urban Estate Phase II Market, Jalandhar, Punjab, India. Contact: jangpreetsingh@yahoo.co.in or +91 98729 79616.

Information you provide

The appointment enquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional phone number and a message. The form prepares a draft in your email application; it does not send the message to this website. Your email provider handles the draft and any message you choose to send. Please do not include medical records, photographs, diagnoses, or other sensitive health information in a website enquiry.

The optional AI clinic information chat sends the messages you enter to the website server and Google Gemini to generate a reply. Do not enter personal, identifying or health information. The website does not intentionally store chat history after the request, but the hosting provider and Google may process technical/request data under their own terms and retention practices.

Third-party services and technical data

Google Maps, Google Fonts, Facebook/Instagram content and externally hosted images or videos may connect your browser directly to those providers. They may receive your IP address, browser/device information, page URL and cookies or similar identifiers. Their own privacy policies apply. Some external media is loaded only when you activate it; the map and fonts may connect on page load.

The site does not include analytics or advertising tags based on the current site files reviewed. The hosting provider may create server logs for security and operation; ask the host for the log fields and retention period.

Purposes and choices

Information is used to display the website, answer a request you choose to send, provide the optional AI response, and protect the service. You can avoid optional chat and embedded media, block third-party resources in your browser, or contact the centre by phone. You can request access, correction or deletion of a message you sent by contacting the centre, subject to applicable law and records the centre must retain.

Children and changes

This site is intended for general visitors, not children. The clinic should publish a child-specific notice and obtain any legally required guardian permissions if it knowingly collects information from children. This policy may be updated when site practices change.

For privacy requests, contact jangpreetsingh@yahoo.co.in. Applicable privacy rights and duties depend on the facts, including where you are located.
