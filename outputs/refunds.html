Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This website does not take bookings or payments. Contact the centre before relying on this page for a particular service or payment. Before paying

Ask the centre for the total price, what is included, any advance or deposit, cancellation and rescheduling deadlines, and the method and timing for any eligible refund. Keep the written quote and payment receipt. Do not pay based solely on website information.

Service complaints
